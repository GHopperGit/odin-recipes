A fish sauce that survied pompeii

Listen to the story here

Ingredients

  • fish intestines
  • salt
  • small fish, like atherine, mullet, or lycostomi

Steps

  1. throw fish intestines into a vessel
  2. salt the intestines
  3. salt the small fish
  4. season the fish in the sun, turning frequently
  5. store for two months
  6. strain and keep the liquid
