Ingredients

  • fillet of a fenny snake
  • eye of newt
  • toe of frog
  • wool of bat
  • toungue of dog
  • adder's fork
  • blind-worm's sting
  • lizard's leg
  • howlet's wing
  • baboon's blood

Steps

  1. Boil water in a cauldron
  2. add the fenny snake, boil and bake
  3. add other ingredients and return to a boil
  4. cool with baboon's blood
